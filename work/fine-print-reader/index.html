Fine Print Reader

A Claude plugin that reads legal documents for you. Send photos of a lease, loan or contract and get back a clean PDF, a plain-language summary and a list of risky clauses, each with the exact quote.

The write-up

3 parts

What it gives back

A clean PDF, with pages cropped, straightened and cleaned of shadows. A plain-language summary: who, what, money, dates, and how to leave. And a list of risky clauses, each with the exact quote, what it means for you, a High, Medium or Low risk level, and a question to ask before signing.

How it works

One skill runs the workflow from photo to report. A Python script cleans the photos with OpenCV and builds the PDF, and a checklist of ten risk types, with extra checks for each kind of document, drives the review.

Worth knowing

It is a reading aid, not legal advice. Laws differ by country and state, and for anything important you should still talk to a lawyer or a legal aid service.
