Ostium

Ostium makes small businesses usable by AI agents. Agents can book, shop and send enquiries for a customer, and a free scan grades any business site on how ready it is for them.

The write-up

4 parts

Why

Customers now ask AI agents to book appointments, compare products and get quotes. Most small business sites are built for people: an agent can read them but cannot act on them, and when it cannot act it moves on to a business where it can. The owner never sees any of it.

What I built

A free scanner that works out what customers mainly do on a site — book, buy or get in touch — and checks whether an agent can do that for them. Behind it sit an MCP server for every business, a booking core, product search with carts that hand off to the shop's own checkout, and enquiries that reach the owner by email.

A detail I like

The database is the source of truth for bookings. A Postgres exclusion constraint stops two bookings landing in the same slot, even when two agents book at the same moment.

The name

Ostium is Latin for doorway. The logo is a doorway, and so is the picture above.
